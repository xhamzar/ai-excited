## The image

A giant humpback whale bursts vertically out of an endless sea of golden
clouds at sunset, caught at the very peak of its breach. The water streaming
off its body turns into a flock of birds mid-air, and a tiny hot air balloon
drifting nearby shows just how enormous the whale is. Deep teal sky above,
molten gold below — a moment that feels impossible, frozen a heartbeat
before the splash.

## How to run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```

`main.py` copies `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`),
creating the directory if needed, and reports the size of the copy.
